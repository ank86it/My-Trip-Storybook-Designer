# DOUBLE-SIDED PRINTING

The PDF must be ordered for normal duplex printing.

The physical sequence should be:

Front side of Sheet 1 → Page 1
Back side of Sheet 1 → Page 2

Front side of Sheet 2 → Page 3
Back side of Sheet 2 → Page 4

Front side of Sheet 3 → Page 5
Back side of Sheet 3 → Page 6

and so on.

Do NOT create a special imposed printing layout unless I specifically request printer-imposition instructions.

The PDF should simply contain the pages in normal sequential order.

My printer will handle duplex printing.

---

# SPIRAL BINDING

The album will be spiral bound after printing.

Therefore:

* Keep important text away from the binding edge.
* Keep faces away from the binding edge.
* Keep important parts of photographs away from the binding edge.
* Maintain a comfortable inner safe margin.
* Do not place page numbers directly against the binding.
* Do not allow speech bubbles or captions to be cut off near the binding.

For landscape pages, assume the LEFT EDGE is the binding edge unless I tell you otherwise.

Maintain a larger safe margin on the left side.

---

# BLEED AND SAFE AREA

If the design intentionally reaches the edge of the paper, extend the artwork appropriately to the page edge.

However, all important content must remain inside the safe area.

Important content includes:

* faces
* text
* speech bubbles
* captions
* dates
* page numbers
* important objects
* photographs
* logos
* location names

Do not allow these elements to be clipped.
